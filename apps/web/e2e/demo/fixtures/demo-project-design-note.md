# Sahyadri Foothills Restoration: Project Design Note (demonstration document)

This is a sample document written for the GroundTruth product demonstration. The statements
below are illustrative project claims of the kind an implementing organisation submits to a
funder. They are not the claims of a registered project.

## Project summary

The project restores degraded hill slopes in the Sahyadri foothills south-west of Pune,
Maharashtra, India, through native-species planting, soil and moisture conservation and
protection from grazing. Field work started in 2021.

## Reported results

1. The project restores 1,900 hectares of degraded land.
2. Vegetation greenness (NDVI) inside the project area increased by 0.05 between 2021 and 2026.
3. Sapling survival three years after planting is 85 percent.
4. The project will sequester 12,000 tonnes of CO2 equivalent over ten years.
5. Bird species richness in the project area has increased by 20 percent since 2021.

## Monitoring

Monitoring uses dry-season satellite imagery (January to March) and annual field surveys of
permanent sample plots.
